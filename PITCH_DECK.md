### Slide 2: The Problem
* **0x Addresses Are Broken for Humans**: Sending funds to `0x2ce45ff1847273f0fd11714744c4f238d004e026` leads to frequent typos, clipboard hijack attacks, and anxiety.
* **No Unified Identity**: Users and developers lack a universal Web3 profile to represent themselves across Botchain dApps.
* **The AI Agent Dilemma**: On Botchain—a network built for the AI economy—autonomous bots and AI agents lack recognizable on-chain handles to transact, communicate, and verify credentials.
* **Centralized DNS Risks**: Traditional domains are subject to arbitrary seizure, ICANN censorship, and high recurring registrar fees.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution — BotNS
* **Human-Readable Handles**: Map complex addresses to simple, memorable `.bot` names (e.g., `satoshi.bot`, `agent.bot`, `pay.bot`).
* **100% On-Chain & Unstoppable**: Every domain, record, and metadata entry is minted directly onto Botchain EVM smart contracts.
* **Native Reverse Resolution**: dApps and wallets can resolve any user's address into their primary `.bot` username automatically.
* **Rich Web3 Profile**: Bind social links (Twitter/X, Telegram, GitHub), avatars, and IPFS/Arweave websites directly to the domain.

### Slide 5: Product Features & Architecture
1. **Instant Search & Availability Engine**: Sub-second availability checks and dynamic tier calculation.
2. **Fair Length-Based Tier Pricing**:

   * 4 Characters (Popular): 10 BOT/yr

4. **Decentralized Website Hosting**: Native IPFS and Arweave hash mapping.
5. **Universal Wallet Connection**: Powered by Reown AppKit, supporting MetaMask, Coinbase, WalletConnect, Rainbow, and Rabby.

### Slide 6: Protocol Economics & Revenue Model
* **Predictable Inflow**: Recurring annual registration and renewal fees paid directly in native `BOT` tokens.
* **Zero Intermediary Risk**: Smart contract automatically routes all proceeds directly to the protocol treasury address upon minting.
* **Anti-Squatting Protection**: Tiered pricing prevents speculative hoarding of short, premium names while keeping standard domains hyper-accessible.
* **Grace Period**: 30-day protected renewal window safeguards domain owners from predatory sniping.
